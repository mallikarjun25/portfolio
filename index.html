About Me

I’m Mallikarjun Aitha, a Data Analytics Engineer with a Master’s in Data Science and years of experience building scalable data pipelines, analytics workflows, dashboards, and reporting systems. I specialize in turning complex, messy data into reliable datasets, automated processes, and clear insights that support better decision-making.

At the City of New Haven, I build and maintain data pipelines, reporting systems, and business intelligence solutions that support operations and capital improvement programs. My work combines data engineering, cloud data platforms, automation, dashboard development, and stakeholder-focused analytics to improve reporting accuracy, efficiency, and visibility across teams.

Before that, I worked across financial services, higher education, and public-sector analytics, developing ETL pipelines, campaign analytics, performance dashboards, and data products used by technical teams, leadership, and operational stakeholders. I’m especially interested in building data systems that are not only technically sound, but also practical, scalable, and genuinely useful for the people making decisions with them.
